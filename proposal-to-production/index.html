From the measurement to a scheduled job, without retyping

What you asked

Roofr takes a job from the measurement report to a signed proposal. Will Salesforce keep that flow, or add double entry?

Our answer Partner build

It keeps the flow and carries it further. QuickMeasure reports fill the quote automatically, and the signed quote becomes the work order, material order and crew schedule on the same record.

The QuickMeasure reader is a Strong CRM build

Cost
QuickMeasure automation is 20 hours. It replaces the Roofr integration line in the SOW, so the total doesn't change.
Still to confirm
Strong CRM will test the QuickMeasure reader on one of your real XML files.

Optional build

Strong CRM connects QuickBooks directly or through Zapier, so customers, jobs and invoices stay in sync with the job. Today, Roofr's QuickBooks Online sync is a one-way beta on the Scale plan.

Ordering from suppliers

Gap

Roofr orders directly from ABC Supply, SRS and QXO. Salesforce has no built-in supplier connection; the material list can be emailed to your supplier rep automatically.

Email handoff is in scope. A direct connection would be quoted separately. Question for you: do you order through Roofr today?
