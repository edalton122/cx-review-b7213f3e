Know the moment a homeowner opens the proposal

What you asked

Roofr shows when a proposal has been viewed. Will we still know when it's opened, clicked or viewed?

Our answer Configured

Yes for sent, viewed and signed. The e-signature tool writes each status and its time back to the quote, and a Flow alerts the rep the moment it's viewed. Neither Roofr nor the e-signature tools document link-click tracking.
