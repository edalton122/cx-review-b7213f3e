A fair comparison

What Roofr does well, and where it stops

Where Roofr is strong

  • Fast, inexpensive measurement reports that flow straight into proposals
  • Polished proposals with options, upgrades and minimum-profit limits
  • Unlimited e-signatures included on paid plans
  • Direct ordering with ABC Supply, SRS and QXO
  • No per-user fees

Why it can't run the whole business

  • No public API for connecting other systems
  • Zapier only sends data out of Roofr, never in
  • Roofr says it doesn't integrate its tools into outside CRMs
  • Can't import other measurement reports, including QuickMeasure
  • QuickBooks sync is a one-way beta on the top plan
