Waste set per material, adjusted for pitch and complexity

What you asked

Roofr applies waste on every proposal line. Can Salesforce set waste per material, and adjust it for steep or cut-up roofs?

Our answer Configured

Yes, and it's already running in our demo org. Each material has its own waste %, a complexity adder covers steep or cut-up roofs, and order quantities round up to whole bundles, rolls and pieces.

The waste worksheet

This works the same way as the quote in our demo org. Change a material's waste % or the roof complexity, and the order quantities update. Measurements and coverages are example values; yours come from QuickMeasure and your catalog.

Order = measured × (1 + waste % + complexity) ÷ coverage, always rounded up. In Salesforce each line's waste starts at the product's default, and reps can change it.
